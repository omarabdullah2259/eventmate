Passionate about building tools that help international students integrate and thrive. EventMate was created to bridge the gap between newcomers and local community events in NRW, Germany — one connection at a time.

What makes EventMate special

Everything you need to discover, join, and connect — in one place.

🔍

Smart Discovery

Search 510+ events by title, city, language or category. Category chips let you filter instantly without reloading.

🤝

Like-Minded Matching

Every event attendee list highlights shared interests and languages — so you know who to say hello to before you arrive.

💬

Group Chat

Every event has its own group chat — only visible to members who joined. Real-time polling keeps the conversation flowing.

📋

Organiser Dashboard

Create and manage your events, track attendees, upload banners, and edit details — all from a clean dashboard.

👤

Student Profiles

Set your university, study program, languages, and interests. Your profile helps others find common ground with you.
