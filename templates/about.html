About EventMate

EventMate is a platform built to help international students across North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, discover local events, connect with like-minded people, and feel at home in a new country.

M
Mohammed Omar Abdullah
Creator & Developer
Passionate about building tools that help international students integrate and thrive. EventMate was created to bridge the gap between newcomers and local community events in NRW, Germany.

🎯 Our Mission

We believe every international student deserves to feel connected. EventMate makes it easy to discover cultural events, workshops, sports meetups, and more — all in one place, with a community of fellow students who share your interests.

✨ Key Features

  • Browse 500+ events across 51 German cities
  • Filter by category, city, and language
  • Join events and see who else is attending
  • Group chat for every joined event
  • Connect with like-minded students
  • Create and manage your own events
  • Personal profile with interests & languages

🛠️ Built With

EventMate is a full-stack web application built with modern tools.

🐍 Python / Flask 🗄️ SQLAlchemy 🗃️ SQLite 🎨 Vanilla CSS ⚡ JavaScript 🔐 Werkzeug Auth

🌍 Who It's For

EventMate is designed for international students studying in North Rhine-Westphalia — but anyone curious about local events in Germany is welcome to explore and join.
